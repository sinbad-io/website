For three years we have used AI as a conversation. You open a chat, explain yourself, get an answer, and it forgets you. That model is ending. The next one is personal. An AI that knows who you are, what you are working on and what you promised last week, and keeps working while you sleep. Instinct.co is the first product I have used that feels like that, and it works because someone decided how everything underneath fits together, and it is fully vertically integrated.

Most agent companies today are not building minds. They are building plumbing. A vector database for memory, a sandbox for code, a workflow engine for crashes, a regular database for everything else. Four systems from four vendors, held together with glue code that breaks every time the models get better. You cannot build something personal out of parts that don't know about each other.

## Latency stopped mattering

Databases were built for people, so we spent decades making them answer in milliseconds. A model takes seconds to think. Nobody will notice your fast database anymore. What agents need instead is to run for days, survive failures, and keep every step they take. A context window is not memory. It is gone the moment the task ends.

Here is the part most people miss. There will be billions of agents, each doing thousands of things a second, and each needs to keep all of it. Nothing we built for SaaS holds up at that scale or that price. The only layer cheap and durable enough is object storage, and everything else has to be rebuilt on top of it.

Every computing era was won by whoever integrated what everyone else kept separate. The PC, the iPhone, now agents. The companies stitching together four systems will lose to the one that makes them one.

## A mind is what it remembers

An agent without memory is a brilliant stranger you meet again every morning. Making it smarter doesn't fix that. Giving it somewhere to live does.

Superintelligence won't be one giant model in a data center. It will be a mind next to every person, one that remembers, acts and gets better every day. What stands between us and that isn't more intelligence. It's memory, a place to act, and time, for the 8 billion agents being built.
